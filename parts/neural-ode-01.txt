          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

        When we ask for times beyond the observation interval, it must extrapolate that fitted function directly.
        It does not explicitly use the idea that the next position should come from the current position and its rate of change.
        For trajectory data, that continuous evolution structure is often exactly what we want to model.
      </div>

      <div class="card panel small">
